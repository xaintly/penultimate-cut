# Privacy

Penultimate Cut collects no data.

- It makes no network connections: no accounts, no analytics, no crash
  reporting, no update checks.
- Your videos, pictures, and projects stay on your computer. The app reads
  the files you import and writes only the projects you save, the videos you
  export, and its own working files (thumbnails, autosaves for crash
  recovery, and settings) in your user folders.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/xaintly/penultimate-cut/issues
